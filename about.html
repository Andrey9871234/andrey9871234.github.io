Our Story: From an Idea to a Movement

Three years ago, a simple idea sparked a mission that would touch hundreds of lives

🌟 How It Started

A Vision Born from Compassion

Three years ago, a group of students in Denmark realized something powerful: the everyday items we take for granted in Europe could be life-changing for children in Moldova.

We discovered that things widely available and affordable in Denmark and across Europe—toys, warm clothes, books, basic necessities—were luxuries for many Moldovan children, especially those in orphanages and from struggling families without parents or stable support.

What started as a conversation among friends became a calling: What if we could bridge this gap? What if we could bring the warmth and joy of Christmas to children who had never experienced it?

"We didn't just want to give gifts. We wanted to share the true spirit of Christmas—the message of love, hope, and the good news of the Gospel. Every child deserves to know they are valued and loved."

— ForaChild Founding Team

Why Moldova?

Moldova is one of Europe's poorest countries, where thousands of children live without basic necessities—and without hope.

📉
26% poverty rate

Over a quarter of the population lives in poverty

👨‍👩‍👧
30,000+ orphaned children

Growing up without parental care

🏚️
Rural families struggle

Many can't afford basic necessities

These aren't just statistics—they're children with dreams, potential, and hearts longing to be seen and loved. We believe God has called us to be His hands and feet in Moldova, bringing both physical gifts and spiritual hope.
