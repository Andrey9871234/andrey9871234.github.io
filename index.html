Why Moldova Needs Our Help

Moldova remains one of Europe's poorest countries, where thousands of children face daily hardships that no child should endure.

📊
26% poverty rate

Over a quarter of the population lives below the poverty line

👶
30,000+ children

Growing up without parental care in institutions or foster homes

🏠
Rural poverty

Over 35% of rural families struggle to afford basic necessities

🎁
Many children

Have never received a Christmas gift or birthday present

Together, we can change this. Every gift, every smile, every moment of joy matters. Your support directly reaches children who need it most.

"Every child deserves to feel loved and special, especially during the holidays."

❤️ We are a group of students from Denmark dedicated to bringing joy to children without parents or from poor families in Moldova
