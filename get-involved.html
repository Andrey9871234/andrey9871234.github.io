Join Our Mission as a Volunteer

From Denmark to Moldova, our volunteers are the heart of ForaChild. Whether you have 2 hours or 20, there's a place for you.

📦

In Denmark

  • Gift sorting and packaging (November-December)
  • Fundraising event organization
  • Social media and marketing support
  • Translation services (Danish/English/Romanian)
  • Photography and videography
  • Administrative support

⏰ Flexible schedule, mostly weekends

Special Opportunity
🇲🇩

In Moldova

  • Gift distribution to children (December)
  • Local coordination and logistics
  • Family visits and needs assessment
  • Translation and cultural guidance
  • Photography and documentation

⏰ 1-2 weeks in December (travel included)

✈️ We cover travel and accommodation for selected volunteers

Remote / Anywhere

  • Graphic design and branding

  • Grant writing and research

Got Questions?

Do I need experience?

Not at all! We provide training and guidance for all roles.

How much time is required?

From 2 hours to full-time—we work with your schedule.

Do I need to speak Romanian?

Helpful but not required. Many of our volunteers are translators!

Can I volunteer as a group?

Yes! We love corporate volunteer days and school groups.
